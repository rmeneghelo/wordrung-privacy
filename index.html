Privacy Policy for Wordrung

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Wordrung ("Application") for mobile devices, published by Droid Craft Works.

1. What Information Does the Application Obtain and How Is It Used?

User Provided Information

The Application does not require registration, does not create an account, and does not ask for your name or email address. The Application has no server of its own: your game progress, unlocked levels, hints and settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

Automatically Collected Information

The Application itself does not collect personal information. The third-party advertising and billing services described in Section 2 may automatically collect device information (such as device model, operating system version and language), a resettable advertising identifier, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads — in order to display ads, prevent fraud, and process purchases.

2. Third-Party Services & SDK Declarations

We use verified third-party SDKs. These services collect and process data in compliance with Google Play Policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): displays the ads that keep the game free. AdMob may collect a resettable advertising identifier, device information, your IP address and information about your interaction with ads. How Google uses information from ads
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchase): processes the optional, one-time "Remove ads" purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — we never receive or store your payment card details. We receive only a purchase confirmation (a purchase token) that unlocks ad-free play.
  • Google Play Core (in-app review): allows the Application to ask you to rate it on Google Play. It is provided by the Play Store and does not send us any personal data.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), a Google consent form is shown before any personalised advertising is served. If you do not consent, ads are served in non-personalised mode. You can change your choice at any time in the Application's settings.

3. Data Retention Policy & Managing Your Information

  • Your game data (progress, settings) stays on your device for as long as the Application is installed. There is no copy on any server of ours.
  • Advertising identifiers are retained by Google according to its own policies. You can reset or delete your resettable advertising identifier at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Security and privacy → Ads).
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required to process payments, verify the unlock and comply with billing regulations.

4. Deleting Your Data (Google Play Compliance)

The Application has no account and no server-side storage, so there is no account on our side to delete. To erase everything the Application stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstall the Application, or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → Wordrung → Storage → Clear data.

For any question or request about data, contact us at rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com with the subject "Data Request". Advertising data held by Google can be managed through the Google settings and policies linked in Section 2.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided us with information without their consent, he or she should contact us at rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com. We will delete such information from our files within a reasonable time.

6. Security

We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Your game data never leaves your device, and the limited data described above is handled by Google's SDKs under their own security practices. Please be aware that, although we endeavor to provide reasonable security, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact us at: rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com
